Our promises

Six pillars, concrete commitments

Listed in the order residents told us they matter.

1

Safety & Security

Top resident priority

Everyone should feel safe in the lift, the basement and at the gate.

  • CCTV at key pointsCameras at the parking, ground floor and terrace, where they matter most.
  • Clear visitor, cab and parking processOne simple procedure, so that no cab or visitor enters without approval.
  • 24×7 security supervisorPlus a digital entry/exit dashboard for better visibility.
  • Fire safety on every floorRegular checks, a volunteer fire warden per floor and cushioning on emergency doors to reduce noise.
  • Lift and emergency readinessReliable lift emergency systems and a block-level emergency response plan.
2

A Transparent & Accountable AOA

You elect us, so you should always know what we are doing and why.

  • Monthly committee meetingsRegular cadence with elected members, plus additional GBMs when needed.
  • Visible expenses & renewalsA simple, shared view of spending and contract renewals.
  • Vendors chosen on valueSelection based on service quality and price, not habit.
  • Audited Assetz handoverAn end-to-end, verified handover from the builder.
  • Resident pollsDecisions backed by quick community polls, through a community app or WhatsApp.
  • A better helpdeskWell-managed helpdesk numbers, so every complaint is logged, tracked and answered.
  • One place for basic infoKey contacts, SOPs, notices and visitor rules, easy to find for owners and tenants.
  • Fair building rulesClear, even-handed checks on illegal constructions and unauthorised modifications.
3

Fair Maintenance Costs

Good service shouldn't mean a heavy bill.

  • Costs on par with other towersWe will benchmark and keep maintenance under control.
  • Better rates through tie-upsPlumbing vendors and summer water-tanker arrangements at or below current market rates.
  • Easier BESCOM name transferHelp for residents with the paperwork.
4

Water & Infrastructure Health

Prevent problems before they become emergencies.

  • Frequent water quality checksRegular testing and a review of treatment efficiency.
  • Proactive plumbing & STP checksScheduled inspections rather than fixes after failure.
  • Monthly safety inspectionsPlay area, railings, nuts and welds on the yoga deck, podium and common balconies.
  • Better connectivityWi-Fi/network coverage in the basement and ground floor.
  • Better roads around usRoad infrastructure needs PWD/panchayat; we will take it up with them and keep you updated.
5

Clean, Green & Accessible

A tower that is pleasant and easy to move around for everyone.

  • Housekeeping SOPDefined cleaning schedules with signed checklists, including floor scrubbing on all floors.
  • More greenery & better lightingPlants in lobbies and podium areas, and motion-sensor lights in stairs and basements.
  • Building aestheticsSmaller touches that make the tower look cared for: greenery, lighting and tidy common areas.
  • Accessibility for allWheelchair access checks, benches on walking paths and a painted safe walkway to the yoga deck.
  • Well-kept children's parkMaintained play equipment and plants.
  • Lobby comforts*Fans, newspapers and a bookshelf in the lobby.
  • Smoke-free bathrooms*A turbo exhaust at the top of the bathroom shaft, to stop cigarette smoke drifting into bathrooms.
  • Dignity for staffBasic amenities such as a table and chairs for housekeeping and house help.
6

Community & Care

Clear rules and warm support, for every household.

  • Clear rules, communicated to allWeekend and common-area do's and don'ts, with the same SOP shared with security, housekeeping and residents.
  • Care for seniorsAn opt-in register of seniors living alone, with emergency contacts at the guard desk.
  • Health & first responseQuarterly health camps (BP, sugar, eyes) and CPR-trained volunteers, with support from nearby hospitals.
  • Welcome kit for newcomersRules, contacts, segregation guide and emergency numbers for new owners and tenants.
  • Festivals & eventsCelebrating our different cultures together.
  • Better amenitiesSmoother clubhouse management and common EV charging stations.
  • Fair policiesA pet-friendly policy, and we will explore the possibility of a designated smoking area.
  • Community melas & pop-up marketsA regular platform for resident entrepreneurs and home-preneurs to showcase their ventures.
  • Ad spaces for residentsIn lifts and on each floor, for resident businesses.
  • Giving backA cloth donation drive for an orphanage.
  • Child care areasWe will explore dedicated child care spaces.
  • Smooth move-in and move-outA simple process for residents and tenants who are joining or leaving.

* Subject to budget availability.
